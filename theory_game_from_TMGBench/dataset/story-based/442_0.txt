Captain Ethan Cruz, an experienced astronaut, has been selected to lead the first manned mission to the distant moon.

As the mission's launch date approaches, both Dr. Sharma and Captain Cruz face crucial decisions that will significantly impact the success of the expedition and their individual careers.

Dr. Sharma must decide whether to share her research data openly with the entire scientific community or keep it confidential, sharing it only with a select few. Sharing openly could accelerate mission preparations and potentially save lives, but it might also allow other scientists to claim credit for her work. Keeping the data confidential would protect her intellectual property but could hinder the mission's progress.
