However, the success of this initiative largely depends on the participation of local physicians.

He's less enthusiastic about remaining independent if the shared EHR is implemented, as he fears being left behind technologically. His least preferred scenario is joining the network if separate systems are maintained, as this would mean losing independence without gaining significant benefits.

The payoff matrix reflects these preferences, with each decision combination resulting in different outcomes for both parties. Dr. Chen and Dr. Johnson must carefully consider their choices, knowing that their decisions will not only affect their own situations but also the broader landscape of healthcare delivery in their community.

As they approach their decision deadline, both Dr. Chen and Dr. Johnson are acutely aware of the potential long-term implications of their choices. The future of healthcare coordination in Central City hangs in the balance, dependent on whether these two influential figures can find a mutually beneficial path forward.
